Pune, in a few lines

Pune sits on the Deccan plateau in Maharashtra, where the Mula and Mutha rivers meet. It was the seat of the Peshwas, and the stone walls of Shaniwar Wada still stand in the old city.

People call it the Queen of the Deccan and the Oxford of the East, because students arrive from everywhere to study here and many never leave.

Today it is old wadas next to new flyovers, hill forts at the edge of town, cafes on FC Road, and traffic that has opinions about everything.

The metro is joining the city together, and the cranes never really leave. That is the Pune this game is built around.

What it is

Deccan Rise: Pune is an open-world game in the spirit of GTA, built around everyday Pune. It is made by Nexora Interactive in Godot 4, and it is still in development.

  • Learning AI traffic

    Every road is a negotiation.

    Traffic driven by learning AI, with the intense congestion and potholed roads Pune is known for.

  • A working metro

    Ride it the way the city does.

    A metro system built to match the real one.

  • A city that changes

    Come back later and the cranes have moved on.

Questions

When can I play it?

The first build (0.1) is planned for 27 March 2027. The game is still in development, so dates can move.

Which part of Pune comes first?

Deccan, Shivajinagar and FC Road make up the first playable area.

Is there a story?

Yes, the game has a working storyline inside the open world.

What is it made with?

Godot 4, with a stylised art style and lightweight performance.
